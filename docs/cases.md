# Real Court Cases (2025-2026)

## 1. Lacey v. State Farm (C.D. Cal., May 2025)
- **Sanction**: $31,100
- **Issue**: Lawyer filed brief with 9 incorrect citations, 2 completely invented (hallucinated)
- **Judge Wilner**: *"The use of AI actively deceived me. I read the brief, was interested in the authorities cited, searched for the decisions... and discovered they DID NOT EXIST. That's frightening."*

## 2. Lexos Media IP v. Overstock.com (D. Kan., Dec 2025)
- **Sanction**: Show cause order, no correction allowed
- **Issue**: Lawyer admitted he didn't verify AI-generated citations
- **Judge Robinson**: Denied request to "redo" the defective brief

## 3. Russell v. Mells (Fla. App., Dec 2025)
- **Sanction**: Referral to Florida Bar (disciplinary investigation)
- **Issue**: Lawyer filed brief with completely invented case ("Cade v. Roberts")
- **Court**: *"No judge on this panel recalls a case where an attorney accidentally filed a completely invented case as legal authority... until generative AI."*
